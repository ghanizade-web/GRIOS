# GRIOS Catalyst-Driven Daily Trading Brief
**Status:** User-approved editorial direction — 2026-11-07 (asset-universe revision)  
**Purpose:** Publish a concise, catalyst-led daily opportunity brief focused exclusively on crypto, FX and commodities.  
**Core principle:** We Track Capital Before The Crowd.

## 1. Editorial objective and eligible universe
The daily output is a trading opportunity brief, not a general market recap. **Equities and individual listed-company shares are excluded** from discovery, ranking, analysis and publication. The eligible universe is:
- **Crypto:** BTC, ETH, liquid altcoins, DeFi, infrastructure tokens, stablecoin/ETF-related instruments where tradable, derivatives and on-chain positioning.
- **FX:** major, minor and selected emerging-market currency pairs; central-bank divergence, rates, inflation, employment, carry, positioning and intervention risk.
- **Commodities:** precious and industrial metals, energy, agricultural commodities, uranium and strategic materials where reliable instruments/prices exist. Include futures, spot references and relevant commodity-linked instruments, clearly distinguishing the instrument type.

Strategic megatrends such as AI, data centers, power, nuclear, defense and semiconductors may be used only as macro-demand drivers for eligible FX/crypto/commodity trades. **Do not recommend or analyze equities as trade candidates.**

## 2. Daily breadth: 10+ items without fabrication
Screen the eligible markets daily with a **target of at least 10 independent market opportunities/watchlist items**, and include more when justified. The target is a coverage target, not permission to invent setups. If fewer than 10 candidates pass minimum evidence and data-quality thresholds, report the actual count and explain the shortfall. Do not pad the report with stale news, duplicate expressions of the same trade, weak technical observations or unsupported narratives.

Separate the list into:
- Top 3 highest-conviction candidates.
- Additional qualified opportunities/watchlist items.
- No-trade / rejected ideas, clearly separated from qualifying opportunities.

## 3. Mandatory structure for each candidate
For each item, include:
- Instrument and direction (LONG/SHORT/WATCH/NO TRADE).
- Latest reliable price or range, quote type, venue/provider and timestamp/time zone where available; state when price is delayed or market is closed.
- Daily/weekly performance only when verified and comparable.
- Catalyst: fresh, specific news/data/filing/policy decision/flow/supply-demand change with publication date and event date.
- Causal chain: catalyst → fundamental transmission → eligible instrument → expected price response.
- Timing/horizon: intraday, 1–5 sessions, 1–4 weeks, or structural.
- Activation: exact observable condition (price/close/volume/breakout/retest/data confirmation). Do not invent levels; use timestamped market data.
- Invalidation: exact evidence or price condition that breaks the thesis.
- Risk label: low/medium/high/speculative, with concise reason.
- Evidence grade: A (primary/official + market confirmation), B (credible secondary + measurable confirmation), C (early/speculative; watch only).

## 4. Selection and scoring
Prioritize:
- Fresh, dated catalyst.
- Clear causal path to the eligible instrument.
- Relative strength/weakness or measurable positioning/flow change.
- Price/volume confirmation or defined confirmation trigger.
- Adequate liquidity and clear invalidation.
- Near-term event or structural catalyst with plausible horizon.
- Cross-market corroboration (rates, yields, dollar, curve, inventories, ETF flows, funding/basis, open interest, liquidations and on-chain data as relevant).

Do not force a trade. A report may contain 10+ WATCH items, but do not label them active trades without confirmation. If no setup passes the evidence threshold, publish “No confirmed trade; monitor only.”

## 5. Catalyst classes to monitor
- Central banks, inflation, employment, rates, yields, currency differentials and intervention.
- Geopolitical shocks, sanctions, trade restrictions, shipping/energy routes and supply disruptions.
- Commodity inventories, production, OPEC decisions, refinery/processing constraints, mine supply, weather and physical premiums.
- Institutional and public positioning: CFTC (with reporting lag), ETF flows, exchange balances, fund disclosures and on-chain flows.
- Crypto-specific events: protocol upgrades, token unlocks, listings/delistings, exploits/hacks and supply overhang, ETF flows, whale flows and derivatives positioning.
- Strategic materials: uranium, copper, lithium, rare earths, gallium, germanium, antimony, tungsten, platinum-group metals and bottlenecks. These are commodity opportunities only where a directly tradable instrument or reliable market reference exists.

## 6. Evidence and language controls
- Separate **Fact / Interpretation / Trade scenario**.
- A whale transfer is not automatically a purchase; custody transfers are not net buying.
- ETF flows, CFTC data and institutional disclosures must be described with true frequency and publication lag.
- Distinguish spot, futures, reference rates, official closes and delayed quotes.
- Conflicting providers or stale prices must be disclosed; never average incompatible quotes.
- Do not state that a news event caused a price move unless timing and mechanism support the inference.
- Do not invent entry, stop, target, return, volume, market cap or order-flow levels.
- A positive catalyst does not automatically justify entering after an extended price move.
- Meme coins, illiquid tokens, leveraged futures and thin commodity contracts must be marked speculative/high risk.
- No unsupported institutional attribution or fabricated “smart money” narrative.
- Never substitute equity shares for an eligible asset class. If an AI/semiconductor/energy theme has no directly tradable crypto, FX or commodity expression with evidence, leave it as context, not a trade.

## 7. Trade expression
Keep the daily brief concise, decisive and Telegram-ready. Example:
**[SYMBOL] — LONG / SHORT / WATCH**  
**Price / timestamp:** [verified quote and type].  
**Why now:** [fresh catalyst].  
**Transmission:** [causal chain].  
**Trigger:** [observable price/volume/data condition].  
**Invalidation:** [condition].  
**Horizon / risk / evidence:** [labels].

Use WATCH when the catalyst is real but price/flow has not confirmed. Use NO TRADE when evidence conflicts or price data is unreliable. Avoid detailed portfolio sizing unless requested.

## 8. Source hierarchy
1. Primary sources: central banks, statistical agencies, regulators, exchanges, official project/protocol releases, commodity agencies and inventory reports.
2. High-quality newswires and financial press: Reuters, Bloomberg, Financial Times, WSJ, AP and comparable sources.
3. Market data providers and specialist sector datasets, with timestamp and methodology disclosed.
4. On-chain analytics and social data as secondary evidence only; cross-check material claims.
5. Social posts and promotional releases are leads, not confirmation.
