# GRIOS Catalyst-Driven Daily Trading Brief
**Status:** User-approved editorial direction — 2026-11-07  
**Purpose:** Replace long daily market summaries with a concise, catalyst-led opportunity brief.  
**Core principle:** We Track Capital Before The Crowd.

## 1. Editorial objective
The daily output is a trading opportunity brief, not a general market recap. It must identify a small number of actionable, evidence-backed setups across FX, crypto, equities, commodities, energy, uranium and strategic materials. The reader should quickly understand what may move, why now, how the catalyst transmits into price, what confirms the trade, and what invalidates it.

## 2. Mandatory structure
1. **Top line:** date, time, market-data cut-off and the single strongest catalyst.
2. **Best setup(s):** normally 1–3 ideas, ranked by evidence and asymmetric opportunity.
3. For each idea, include:
   - Instrument and direction (long/short/watch/no trade).
   - Catalyst: dated, specific news, data, filing, policy decision, corporate action, flow or supply/demand change.
   - Causal chain: catalyst → fundamental transmission → asset/sector → expected price response.
   - Timing/horizon: intraday, 1–5 sessions, 1–4 weeks, or structural.
   - Activation: exact observable condition (price/close/volume/breakout/retest/data confirmation). Do not invent levels; cite timestamped market data.
   - Invalidation: exact evidence or price condition that breaks the thesis.
   - Risk label: low/medium/high/speculative, with a concise reason.
   - Evidence grade: A (primary/official + market confirmation), B (credible secondary + measurable confirmation), C (early/speculative; watch only).
4. **Catalyst calendar:** only the 2–5 events likely to move the selected instruments.
5. **No-trade / disconfirming evidence:** one short line where a popular narrative is not yet tradeable.
6. **Sources and timestamp:** citations in research/audit metadata; keep raw URLs and English RSS excerpts out of Telegram copy.
7. **Disclaimer and social footer** for publication-ready versions.

## 3. Selection and scoring
Prioritize setups with:
- A fresh, dated catalyst (not recycled narrative).
- A clear causal path to the instrument.
- Relative strength/weakness or a measurable positioning/flow change.
- Price/volume confirmation or a defined confirmation trigger.
- Adequate liquidity and a clear invalidation condition.
- A near-term event or structural catalyst with a plausible time horizon.

Do not force a daily trade. If no setup passes the evidence threshold, publish “No confirmed trade; monitor only.” Prefer 1–3 strong ideas over a long symbol list.

## 4. Catalyst classes to monitor
- Central banks, inflation, employment, rates, yields and currency differentials.
- Geopolitical shocks, sanctions, trade restrictions and shipping/energy routes.
- Corporate earnings, guidance, capex, contracts, M&A, regulatory filings and insider/ownership disclosures.
- Smart-money and institutional positioning: CFTC (with reporting lag), 13F (quarterly/lagged), ETF flows, exchange balances, on-chain flows and public fund disclosures.
- AI infrastructure: semiconductors, memory, networking, data centers, power generation, grid equipment, cooling and storage.
- Strategic materials: uranium, copper, lithium, rare earths, gallium, germanium, antimony, tungsten, platinum-group metals and supply-chain bottlenecks.
- Crypto-specific events: protocol upgrades, token unlocks, exchange listings/delistings, exploit/hack supply overhang, ETF flows, whale flows and derivatives positioning.

## 5. Evidence and language controls
- Separate **Fact / Interpretation / Trade scenario**.
- A whale transfer is not automatically a purchase; exchange withdrawals/deposits may be custody transfers.
- ETF flows, CFTC data, 13F filings and institutional ownership must be described with their true frequency and publication lag.
- Distinguish spot, futures, reference rates, official closes and delayed quotes.
- Conflicting providers or stale prices must be disclosed; never average incompatible quotes.
- Do not state that a news event caused a price move unless the timing and mechanism support that inference.
- Do not invent entry, stop, target, return, volume, market cap or order-flow levels.
- A positive fundamental catalyst does not automatically justify buying after an extended price move.
- High-volatility meme coins and junior miners must be marked speculative/high risk.
- No unsupported institutional attribution or fabricated “smart money” narrative.

## 6. Trade expression
Keep the daily brief short and decisive. Example:
**[SYMBOL] — LONG / SHORT / WATCH**  
**Why now:** [fresh catalyst].  
**Transmission:** [causal chain].  
**Trigger:** [observable price/volume/data condition].  
**Invalidation:** [condition].  
**Horizon / risk / evidence:** [concise labels].

Use “watch” when the catalyst is real but the chart/flow has not confirmed. Use “no trade” when evidence conflicts or price data is unreliable. Avoid detailed portfolio sizing unless the user requests it.

## 7. Source hierarchy
1. Primary sources: central banks, statistical agencies, regulators, company filings, official press releases, exchange notices and project operators.
2. High-quality newswires and financial press: Reuters, Bloomberg, Financial Times, WSJ, AP and comparable sources.
3. Market data providers and specialist sector datasets, with timestamp and methodology disclosed.
4. On-chain analytics and social data as secondary evidence only; cross-check material claims.
5. Social posts and promotional releases are leads, not confirmation.

## 8. Daily quality gate
Before release:
- [ ] 1–3 highest-quality opportunities are ranked; no filler.
- [ ] Every setup has a fresh catalyst and explicit causal chain.
- [ ] Every actionable idea has a trigger and invalidation.
- [ ] Market data is timestamped and its quote type is identified.
- [ ] Flow data is not misrepresented as executed buying/selling.
- [ ] Contradictory evidence is shown.
- [ ] No invented prices, levels, targets or institutional activity.
- [ ] The report is concise Persian and Telegram-ready.
- [ ] If nothing qualifies, say so explicitly.
