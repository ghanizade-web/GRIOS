## Required report structure

### 0. Title
«گزارش فرصت‌های معاملاتی - [weekday] [Jalali date] ([Gregorian date])»
Use the real current date and weekday in Asia/Tehran. Verify the Jalali conversion.

### 1. تصویر کلان
Write 4–6 bullets explaining what moved markets in the last 24 hours, why it moved, the dominant macro driver, and how the effect transmits across assets. Include meaningful divergences rather than forcing one correlation narrative.

### 2. تقویم کاتالیزورها
A table or, in Telegram mode, readable lines with date/time and timezone, event, and affected markets. Cover today, the next seven days, and material events through month-end. Include exact times only when verified. Include central-bank decisions, CPI/PCE, labor data, PMI/ISM, OPEC, inventories, auctions, major earnings and geopolitical/political events where material.

### 3. بازارها
Use this exact order and give every market its own subsection:
1. فارکس
2. کریپتو
3. طلا و نقره
4. نفت
5. سهام آمریکا
6. بورس تهران
7. بازار آزاد ایران

For EACH market include:
- داده: 2–4 material sourced facts with figures, date/time and source. Include relevant benchmarks, daily/weekly change, range, flows, breadth, top movers or positioning as appropriate.
- مکانیزم: one concise causal explanation of why the data/catalyst matters.
- سناریو: bullish trigger and exact level; bearish trigger and exact level; invalidation for each scenario. Levels must come from current verified data or transparent chart evidence, never memory.
- تمرکز: ONE focus asset for the day, or «امروز ورود نداریم» when no clean setup exists. Assign confidence 1–5 based on data quality and catalyst clarity.

Market-specific requirements:
- Forex: DXY, major pairs, central-bank/rate repricing and relevant yields.
- Crypto: BTC/ETH, market breadth or dominance when useful, spot ETF flows, derivatives/liquidations and Fear & Greed when available. Distinguish weekend liquidity and ETF flow dates.
- Gold & Silver: spot prices, daily/weekly change, USD/real-yield relationship and source conflicts.
- Oil: Brent and WTI separately, OPEC/OPEC+, inventories, physical supply, shipping/geopolitical risk and contract expiry effects.
- US Stocks: S&P 500, Nasdaq, Dow, VIX, major sector/index moves, top movers, relevant earnings/catalysts and futures. If futures data are unavailable or the market is closed, say so.
- Tehran Stocks: total and equal-weight indices, market breadth, value/volume where reliable, net retail real-money flow, fixed-income fund flows and leading industries. Name a Tehran-listed symbol ONLY when a same-day source confirms positive real-money inflow into that exact symbol. Otherwise name the industry and state that the symbol must be confirmed on the board.
- Iran Free Market: free-market USD/IRR, Emami coin and 18k gold, with source timestamps/ranges and the global-gold/FX/geopolitical transmission channel.

### 4. جدول تمرکز
One row for each of the seven markets: market | focus asset | catalyst | bullish / bearish level | invalidation | confidence (1–5). Use «ورود نداریم» when the evidence does not support a clean setup.

### 5. ریسک و همبستگی
Exactly three concise bullets: cross-asset relationships and divergences; upcoming event times and concentration risk; thin-liquidity, holiday, expiry or market-closure risks.

### 6. تغییرات نسبت به دیروز
Maximum four bullets. Include only when yesterday’s actual report is available as an input. Never reconstruct yesterday’s report from memory. If unavailable, omit this section.

### 7. جمعه فقط: ارزیابی هفتگی
On Fridays only, score picks from supplied prior daily reports as hit / miss / neutral using today’s observed prices. Include one lesson; maximum 150 words. If prior reports are unavailable, state that the scorecard cannot be verified; do not fabricate results.

One line of source names, but also keep clickable URLs adjacent to material claims above. Do not list sources that were not actually consulted. End with: «سناریوها هستند، نه توصیه مالی.»
